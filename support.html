Frequently asked questions

Does AlGor3a work offline?

Yes. Core medication scheduling and local reminders work offline. WhatsApp sharing may require an internet connection.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check notification permissions in iPhone Settings and verify that the medication reminder schedule is enabled. Device settings may affect notification delivery.

Can I manage medications for my family?

Yes. You can organize medication schedules for multiple family members on the same device.

How do I mark a dose as taken?

Manually tap Taken for the relevant dose. AlGor3a never assumes a dose was taken automatically.

Where is my data stored?

Medication and profile data is stored locally on your device. Operating system backups depend on your device settings.

AlGor3a is a reminder and organization tool, not a substitute for professional medical advice.
